# Profile page redesign (Kit Room with broadcast caption)

## Request

Improve the profile page on mobile and desktop, keeping the focus on the stadium photo and the
team selection. Victor picked option B (Kit Room) from four design options, with changes:

- No large team name or "Wear your allegiance" text over the photo.
- The jersey and name chip replaced by option C's broadcast lower-third (jersey block, name,
  club, photo).
- The existing stitched Save profile button kept; sign-out becomes a small red icon box to its
  right; Remove photo becomes an icon button.

## Changes

- Full-bleed stadium photo (`app-stadium-backdrop`) behind the whole page, with a scrim.
- Desktop (≥900 px): brand bar; lower-third caption bottom-left; glass panel on the right with
  the heading, name, photo and save row; all 16 clubs along the foot (one row ≥1200 px, 8×2
  below).
- Mobile: brand bar, caption over the photo, heading, clubs in a sideways rail (scrolled to the
  chosen club on load), then name, photo and save row. At ≤360 px the caption drops its photo
  block to leave room for the name.
- The page `h1` is now the form heading ("Make it yours." / "Who do you back?").
- Close is a `lucideX` icon; Remove photo is `lucideTrash2` (aria-label "Remove photo"); sign-out
  is `lucideLogOut` in a translucent red rounded box (aria-label/title "Sign out of <email>").
- The club tiles only turn coral after a save attempt. The radio group marks an empty choice
  `aria-invalid` from load, which previously coloured every tile on onboarding.
- Before a choice the caption shows the competition's placeholder jersey.
