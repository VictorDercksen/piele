# Watch video button: look and playback on the iPhone

## Request

On a duty card the "Watch video" button under an evidence entry looked clipped, and tapping it
did nothing on the iPhone. Fix both. The 50 MB evidence limit stays (the Supabase Free plan
caps uploads at 50 MB).

## Cause

- Look: `duty-card.scss` set `.evidence-trail .text-button { padding: 0 }`, which beat the
  shared white pill's `padding-inline: 16px` (`ui.scss`), so the label and play icon ran into
  the rounded, stitched edge.
- Playback: both `DutyCard.watch` and `EvidenceReview.watch` awaited the signed playback URL
  and only then called `window.open(url, '_blank', 'noopener')`. Safari blocks a tab opened
  after an await because the tap no longer counts as the reason for it, so nothing happened.

## Completed

- `core/league/duties/playback-tab.ts`: `openPlaybackTab(playbackUrl)` opens the tab during
  the tap, clears its opener, then loads the signed URL into it; on failure it closes the tab
  and rethrows so the caller's error card shows. Where pop-ups are blocked outright it opens
  the video in the current tab.
- `DutyCard.watch` and `EvidenceReview.watch` use it.
- `duty-card.scss`: the evidence trail's button keeps its side padding (only the block padding
  is removed), is 34 px tall and sits 6 px below the note.
- Specs: `playback-tab.spec.ts` (tab opens before the URL arrives; closed on failure);
  `duty-card.spec.ts` stubs `window.open` and checks the tab closes on a failed URL.
