# Weather-styled kickoff forecast

## Request

Style the match centre's kickoff forecast background by the weather, similar to Apple Weather, after researching how other apps do it on Mobbin.

## Research (Mobbin, iOS)

- Apple Weather: full-bleed sky per condition and time of day (grey cloud layer for cloudy, blue for sunny, dark for night), with translucent rounded tiles and large thin temperature.
- Box Box Club: race weather card with a gradient sky and icon, big temperature, stat grid below.
- Tide Guide and (Not Boring) Weather: gradient backgrounds keyed to conditions; illustrated weather over a coloured sky.

## Completed

- API: `weather.fetch_forecast` also requests Open-Meteo `is_day` and returns `isDay` (bool or null). `tests/test_matches.py` covers it.
- Web: `features/match/weather-sky.ts` maps the WMO code to a scene (clear, partly, cloudy, fog, drizzle, rain, snow, storm) and day or night, with a Lucide icon. Without `isDay` (snapshots cached before this change) it falls back to the SAST kickoff hour (06:00 to 19:00 is day). Unit tests in `weather-sky.spec.ts`.
- `match.page`: the weather panel gets `has-sky`, `data-sky` and `data-time` when the forecast is `ok`, a decorative `aria-hidden` sky layer (sun or moon and stars, blurred drifting clouds, rain streaks, snow, a faint storm flash), a condition icon, a larger temperature and dark glass fact tiles. Unavailable, too early and past states keep the plain panel.
- Animations stop under `prefers-reduced-motion` through the existing global rule. Precipitation fades out towards the bottom so the source line stays readable.
- `e2e/match-centre.spec.ts` asserts the rainy night sky for the mocked fixture.
