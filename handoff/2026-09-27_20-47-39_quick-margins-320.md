# Quick margins fit a 320 px phone

## Request

CI failed on PR #53 after PR #54 (the quick margins) was merged into its branch: the
season-timeline e2e "keyboard timeline and playoff layout on a phone" found the match page
scrolling sideways at 320 px.

## Completed

- `apps/web/src/app/features/match/picks-panel/picks-panel.scss`: under the 480 px container
  query the quick chips are 30 px wide with 3 px gaps and the Draw chip's padding drops to
  12 px, so three chips a side (the 21s were already hidden) fit beside Draw in a 320 px
  viewport (96 px against about 103 px a side); the margin field is `width: 100%` capped at
  104 px so it shrinks with its column rather than overflowing it.

  on 4340: `season-timeline`, `superbru`, `match-centre` and `profile-and-layout` specs,
  25 passed, the failing test among them.
