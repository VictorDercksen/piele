# Home page: the kickoff ruler in its own band

Recorded on 2026-10-01 in Africa/Johannesburg time. Branch `claude/home-pick-band`, from `master` at `88fe677` (PR #92 merged).

## Request

On a phone the kickoff ruler sat between the stadium and the match-centre button, so it read as part of the featured match rather than the round. Four separation options were mocked up; the user chose the band: the ruler in its own strip under the match footer, with the label kept as `YOUR PICKS · …` and no round prefix.

## Changes

- `match-hero.html`: the ruler block leaves `.match-footer` and becomes a sibling `.pick-band` after it. The footer no longer takes a `ruled` class, so it is exactly as it was before PR #92: stadium on the left, button on the right, stacked on phones.
- `match-hero.scss`: `.pick-band` has a top rule, a darker ground and a faint inset shadow; the `.match-footer.ruled` rules and the stacked-footer overrides are gone. The ruler keeps its sideways scroll where the band is narrower than the kickoffs.

- The dot of the fixture the hero shows wears a chalk ring with a dark gap (`.dot.featured`) and carries `aria-current="true"`, so the band says which match the page is looking at. The dots sit 6 px apart so the ring clears its neighbours.

No change to the view builder, the home page or the unit specs. The kickoff journey in `e2e/superbru.spec.ts` now also checks the featured dot's ring and `aria-current`.
