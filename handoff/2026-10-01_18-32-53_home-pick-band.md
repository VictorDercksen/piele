# Home page: the kickoff ruler in its own band

Recorded on 2026-10-01 in Africa/Johannesburg time. Branch `claude/home-pick-band`, from `master` at `88fe677` (PR #92 merged).

## Request

On a phone the kickoff ruler sat between the stadium and the match-centre button, so it read as part of the featured match rather than the round. Four separation options were mocked up; the user chose the band: the ruler in its own strip under the match footer, with the label kept as `YOUR PICKS · …` and no round prefix.

## Changes

- `match-hero.html`: the ruler block leaves `.match-footer` and becomes a sibling `.pick-band` after it. The footer no longer takes a `ruled` class, so it is exactly as it was before PR #92: stadium on the left, button on the right, stacked on phones.
- `match-hero.scss`: `.pick-band` has a top rule, a darker ground and a faint inset shadow; the `.match-footer.ruled` rules and the stacked-footer overrides are gone. The ruler keeps its sideways scroll where the band is narrower than the kickoffs.

- The dot of the fixture the hero shows wears a chalk ring with a dark gap (`.dot.featured`) and carries `aria-current="true"`, so the band says which match the page is looking at.

No change to the view builder, the home page or the unit specs. The kickoff journey in `e2e/superbru.spec.ts` now also checks the featured dot's ring and `aria-current`.

## Follow-up from a phone screenshot of Round 1

The user's Round 1 on master showed eight missed rings (no picks recorded) and the "NOW" pin overflowing at the right. Changes on this branch:

- `kickoffRuler` returns null for a finished round (every kickoff passed, no `TBC` fixture) with no pick of the member's, so the band is not drawn at all.
- `pin` is null once every kickoff has passed: the pin only stands before the next kickoff. The view's `now` field is gone, and the pin reads just `NOW` without the clock time it repeated.
- The ruler has 5 px of top padding (axis at 18 px) so the featured ring is not clipped by its scroll box. Dots sit 5 px apart, slots have 7 px padding.
- Below 480 px of container width: 24 px dots, 4 px gaps, 6 px slot padding. Measured with Chromium at 844 px tall: no ruler overflow at 375 and 390 px, before or after the round; 44 px of sideways scroll at 320 px.
- Specs: two new ruler cases (pin dropped after the last kickoff; null for a finished round without picks, kept open by a `TBC` fixture). The kickoff journey expects `NOW` alone and no pin on finished Round 2.

## The match-centre button

The user reported that "Enter the match centre" does not work on their phone. Not reproduced: in Chromium with iPhone 13 emulation and touch, on master's dev build, on master's production build served with the `vercel.json` headers, and on this branch, a tap at the position from the screenshot hits the button and opens `/piele/match/292585?round=1`. `elementFromPoint` across the button returns the button; the mobile navigation's box ends 200 px below it. WebKit is not installed in this container, so Safari was not tested. This branch moves the ruler out of the button's footer, which removes the one structural change #92 made near the button.
