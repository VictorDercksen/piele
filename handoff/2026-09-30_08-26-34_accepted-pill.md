# Duty card: decision as a Spartan pill

## Request

Show "Accepted" in the duty card's submission line as a styled pill using Spartan, then open a PR.

## Changes

- Generated the Spartan Helm badge (`ng g @spartan-ng/cli:ui badge`): `src/app/shared/ui/badge/`, path alias `@spartan-ng/helm/badge` in `tsconfig.json`. The generator's incidental edits to `package.json`, `package-lock.json` and `tsconfig.app.json` were reverted.
- `duty-card.html`: the decision (`Accepted`, `Rejected`, `Pending`, `Superseded`) is a `hlmBadge` (outline variant) after the submission time; the trailing `·` separator is gone.
- `duty-card.ts`: imports `HlmBadge`.
- `duty-card.scss`: `.decision` is a small uppercase pill (999px radius) tinted from a `--decision` colour: green for accepted, coral-red for rejected, accent for pending, muted otherwise.

- `ng build` (Node 24 via `npx node@24`): success, no warnings.
- `ng test --watch=false`: 558 passed.
- Screenshots of the Piele Round 01 league duties at 320 and 390 px: pill renders, no horizontal overflow.
