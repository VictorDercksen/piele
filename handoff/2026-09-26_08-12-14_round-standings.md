## Completed

- Migration `supabase/migrations/20260926080000_round_standings.sql`: `piele.round_standings` (one row per season member and round, `points numeric(6,1)`, recorder, version), same-league composite foreign keys, league-scoped RLS for `piele_api`. Ranks are not stored.
- API: `GET /v1/standings?round=` (members) and `PUT /v1/rounds/{round}/standings` (captain; replaces the round's table, members left out lose their row, unchanged tables write nothing). Ranks are derived on read: tied points share a rank and the next rank skips; ties list alphabetically. A change writes a `standings.recorded` audit event and a `standings_recorded` feed entry ("Round 01 Superbru standings updated.", detail names the leader). `service.round_label()` extracted from `duty_title()`.
- Web: `HttpLeagueData` loads `/v1/standings` with the other league records and clears it on sign-out. The existing standings page and home leaderboard already render `RoundStanding`. Empty-state text now says the captain records the pool results.
- Tests: two API tests (recording, ranks with ties, member read, removal, idempotence, feed; validation: unknown and duplicate members, 0.05 precision, negatives, round 22). One `HttpLeagueData` spec.
- Docs: README, apps/api/README.md, apps/api/CLAUDE.md, apps/web/CLAUDE.md.
