# Match chat in the member's club colours

## Request

Style the match chat after the member's favourite team. Five options were mocked up
(https://claude.ai/artifact/PSe3rud8dLtyyr7eHoVypX); the member chose option E, the supporter's
scarf with the banner behind their own questions, with the tint over the banner reduced slightly.

## Changes

- `features/match/match-chat/match-chat.club.ts` (+ spec) and `match-chat.models.ts`: `chatClub`
  turns the favourite team and its URC banner into `ChatClub` (colour, accent, banner colour,
  pattern); null without a favourite team.
- `match-chat-launcher.ts` / `.html` / `.scss`: reads `ProfileService.team()` and the competition's
  banners into `club`, binds `--club` and `--club-accent` on the button and passes `club` to the
  sheet. With a club the disc is the club colour inside a striped ring (colour, chalk, accent).
- `match-chat-sheet.ts` / `.html` / `.scss`: optional `club` input. With a club the sheet carries
  `data-club` and the `--club*` properties, a 12 px scarf stripe replaces the teal top border (it
  also tops the desktop side panel), the teal becomes the club accent through `--chat-accent`, the
  send disc is the club colour, and the member's questions show the banner pattern as an `<img>`
  under a dark club-colour tint (42% to 26% club colour; the mock-up used 55% to 35%). Without a
  club everything stays teal as before.
- `match-chat-launcher.spec.ts`: a `ProfileService` stub, a case for a club and one without.
- `apps/web/CLAUDE.md`: the match chat row mentions the club styling.
