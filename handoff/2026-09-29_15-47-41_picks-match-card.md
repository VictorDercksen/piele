# Pick form as a match card

## Request

Victor wanted the pool picks panel to look less bland without changing how picks work. He reviewed five mockups and picked option A, "Match card", without its large "<team> by X" readout.

## Changes

The form is now laid out top to bottom as:

- A header row with the "Your pick" label on the left and the kickoff lock time on the right. The lock time used to sit in the note at the bottom.
- The matchup: one crest button per side, showing the crest, the name and Home or Away.
  - Tapping a crest still moves the pick one point toward that side.
  - The chosen side gets an underline in the club's accent colour.
  - The other side fades only once a club is picked. A draw or no pick leaves both at full strength.
- The scale runs the full width with no crest tabs. The tick labels sit under the strip.
  - The fill still uses the club's banner colour.
  - The marker is a chalk tag with the club's accent colour along its bottom edge.
- The quick margins and Draw form one centred row. Each quick margin has a thin line in its club's colour and fills with that colour when pressed.
- The margin field sits in a fixed position with − and + buttons on either side.
  - The buttons (`stepMargin`) keep the side and stay between 1 and 150.
  - They are disabled until a club is picked.
  - Save pick sits on the same row, followed by Cancel when editing.
- "Make your pick to see the pool's picks." sits at the bottom with an eye-off icon, and shows only until a pick is recorded.

Removed:

- `marginSlot`. The margin field no longer moves to the chosen side of Draw.

Unchanged:

- The saved-pick strip (`.mine`), the pool's split and the pool table.
