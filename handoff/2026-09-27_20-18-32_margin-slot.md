## Request

On the margin scale (handoff `2026-09-27_20-01-36`, PR #52): remove the "Reads {club} by n"
line, fix the Draw button in place, and let the margin box switch between the left and the
right of the Draw box according to the chosen side. A new pull request, stacked on PR #52.

## Completed

- `apps/web/src/app/features/match/picks-panel/picks-panel.html`: the reading line is gone.
  Under the strip, the Draw chip sits fixed under the middle tick (the crest ends are equal, so
  the strip's centre is the track's) and the "Margin (points)" field sits beside it, left of
  the chip for the home side and right (`.margin-field.away`) for the away side.
- `picks-panel.ts`: `marginSlot`, a signal of `'home' | 'away'`, set from the side control's
  changes whenever a club is chosen, so the disabled field stays on the last club's side while
  a draw is chosen and starts on the home side. `ScaleView.hint` removed; `text` stays for the
  range's `aria-valuetext`.
- `picks-panel.scss`: `.scale-under` is a three-column grid (`1fr auto 1fr`), the chip in the
  middle column and the field in column 1 or 3, both pinned to row 1 (the chip comes first in
  the flow, and grid auto-placement otherwise dropped the column-1 field to a second row). The
  reading styles are gone.
- `picks-panel.spec.ts` and `e2e/superbru.spec.ts`: the reading assertions became
  `aria-valuetext` checks; the spec checks the field's side through the `away` class, and the
  e2e compares the field's and the chip's bounding boxes as the pick moves from Stormers to
  Sharks and back.
- `apps/web/CLAUDE.md`: the picks panel sentence updated.
