# Info pills on the pick form and forecast

## Request

Turn the pick form's "Picks lock at kickoff" line, the "The pool's picks show here at kickoff" line and the forecast's source line into styled pills with icons, like the other pills on the match page, and show a screenshot.

## Changes

- Pick form: the lock line and the pool line are `hlmBadge.status-pill` (muted tone) with their existing Lucide icons (`lucideLock`, `lucideUsers`). The trailing full stops are gone. Both pills may wrap at 320 px instead of clipping.
- Forecast: the source line is now a row of muted status pills: venue (`lucideMapPin`), forecast hour (`lucideClock`), provider (`lucideRadar`) and, when known, "Updated …" (`lucideRefreshCw`, now with the zone like the teamsheets "Checked" pill). The unavailable state shows the provider pill alone.
